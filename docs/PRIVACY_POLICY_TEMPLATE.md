## Information the App stores on your device

The App stores your game progress, statistics, achievements, settings and
any game in progress **only on your device**. We do not operate servers for
the App, do not require an account, and do not receive this information.
Android's system backup, if you have enabled it, may include this data in
your personal device backup managed by Google under your Google account.

You can delete this information at any time in the App (**Settings → Reset
all progress**) or by uninstalling the App.

## Advertising

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to
prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information
such as:

- your device's advertising ID and app set ID,
- your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location,
- interactions with ads and the App (for example app launches and ad taps),
- diagnostic and performance information.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United
Kingdom and Switzerland, and some US states), the App asks for your consent
or choices through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown.
You can change your choices at any time in **Settings → Ad privacy choices**
(when available in your region). You can reset or delete your advertising ID
in your device's settings.

Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy> and in "How Google uses information
from sites or apps that use our services":
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

## Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play under
Google's terms; we do not receive your payment details. The App only stores
on your device that ads were removed, and asks Google Play to restore the
purchase after a reinstall.

## Permissions

The App requests internet access only to load ads and to talk to Google Play for purchases. If you turn on the daily
reminder, it asks for permission to show notifications; the reminder is
scheduled on your device and nothing is sent to us. Sharing a result only
happens when you tap Share, and you choose where it goes. The App does not
access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
